About the Bureau

Safe, accessible and modern transport for every community

The Bureau plans, builds and maintains the regional road network, regulates public and freight transport, and works with zones and woredas to bring year-round road access to rural communities across South Ethiopia.

Vision

To see a region connected by a safe, efficient and sustainable road and transport network.

Mission

Deliver quality roads and reliable transport services through good governance, technology and community participation.
